The rules, for every Spex app

Read-only, always

Each app runs on a read-only Kalshi API key and has no order code. Nothing under the Spex name places, amends, or cancels a bet.

Your data stays yours

Apps talk straight to Kalshi. No Spex server in the middle, no account to create, nothing about your positions leaves your machine.

Common questions
Is Spex affiliated with Kalshi?

No. Spex is an independent open-source project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Kalshi. "Kalshi" is their trademark.

Does Spex work with Polymarket, FanDuel, DraftKings, or other sportsbooks?

No. Every Spex app reads from Kalshi's API and nothing else. Other prediction markets and sportsbooks have their own APIs or none at all, and there's no plan to add them.

Can any Spex app place a bet for me?

No. They run on read-only Kalshi API keys and have no order code. They show you what you hold and what it's worth.

Everything under the Spex name is free and stays that way. If one of these apps earns its keep, there's a Ko-fi. Tips cover the Apple developer account that keeps the Mac app signed and notarized.
